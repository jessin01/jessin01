👋 For grown-ups

A little note for the big people

🫱

Built for little hands

Every button is oversized and forgiving, there are no menus to get lost in, and nothing here can navigate away from the page.

🔇

Sound you control

Voices use your device's own speech, so nothing is downloaded. One tap on Sound on / off at the top mutes the whole playhouse.

🕊️

No ads, no tracking

No accounts, no adverts, no data collected, no links out to the wider internet. Just a quiet corner to play in.

⏳

Best in small doses

Ten happy minutes beats an hour. Play alongside where you can — say the letters together and it counts double.
